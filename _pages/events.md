Below is a list of events (schools, conferences, minisymposia/dedicated sessions at larger events, etc.), that I have participated in organizing over the years.

## Geilo Winter Schools in Computational Sciences

I am part of the organizing committee of the [Geilo Winter Schools in Computational Sciences](https://sintefmath.github.io/geilo-winter-school/), an annual meeting place for PhD students, Postdocs, and young researchers. Hosted at the historical Dr. Holms Hotel, the school gathers leading experts to lecture on emerging topics in computational sciences.

### Recent Winter Schools:

**Geilo Winter School 2025: Inverse Problems** (main organizer)  
*January 19-24, 2025, Geilo, Norway*  
The 25th Geilo Winter School focused on Inverse problems, with topics ranging from solving inverse problems with Julia's SciML, water waves, geosteering, and machine learning. Lecturers: Adrian Kirkeby (Simula), Chris Rackauckas (JuliaHub, PumasAI, MIT), Sergey Alyaev (NORCE), Giovanni Alberti (University of Genoa).

**Geilo Winter School 2024: Graphs and Applications** (co-organizer)  
*January 21-26, 2024, Geilo, Norway*  
The 24th Geilo Winter School took a dive into the world of graphs and their applications, covering topics from how to best model graphs in Python to spectral theory to graph neural networks.

**Geilo Winter School 2023: Computational Statistics** (co-organizer)  
*January 22-27, 2023, Geilo, Norway*  
The 23rd Geilo Winter School covered the topic of computational statistics, which is of great importance in a number of modern applications. The lectures ranged from introductions to Monte Carlo methods, MCMC and friends, Bayesian inference and Integrated Nested Laplace Approximation to using Julia for computational statistics and even covered some reinforcement learning.

**Geilo Winter School 2022: Continuous Optimization** (co-organizer)  
*January 24-28, 2022, Geilo, Norway*  
Continuous optimization is the study of maximizing functions of continuous variables. In the school of 2022 we looked into a number of exciting topics ranging from continuous optimization basics to topics like shape optimization, PDE-constrained optimization, and the relationship between machine learning and optimization.

**Geilo Winter School 2021: Explainable Algorithms** (co-organizer)  
*January 25-29, 2021, Geilo, Norway*  
The past decade has seen impressive developments in powerful machine learning algorithms. In the 2021 winter school, we took a deep dive into explainable algorithms, focusing on proper understanding of what the algorithms are in fact telling us, particularly in numerical simulation applications.

**Geilo Winter School 2020: Modern Techniques and Algorithms in High-Performance Computing** (co-organizer)  
*January 19-24, 2020, Geilo, Norway*  
This winter school looked at important topics related to HPC, including GPU computing, Quantum computing, Automatic differentiation, and Distributed parallelism.

## Conference Sessions and Symposia

**Dedicated Session: Direct Heat Geothermal Energy: Importance of Forward Modelling and Data Assimilation**  
*Co-organizer with D. Voskov*  
85th EAGE Annual Conference & Exhibition, June 2024, Oslo, Norway  
Heating and cooling demands almost 50% of the EU's total gross energy consumption. In this session, we discussed how numerical modeling and data assimilation can help in improving the energy production from direct heat systems.

**Minisymposium: Predictive Digital Twins**  
*Co-organizer with T. Kvamsdal, K. Mathisen*  
9th European Congress on Computational Methods in Applied Sciences and Engineering, June 3-7, 2024, Lisbon, Portugal  
This minisymposium had a special emphasis on enabling technologies for Digital Twins, focusing on virtual representations of physical assets enabled through data and simulators for real-time prediction, optimization, monitoring, control, and decision-making.

**Minisymposium: Practical Simulation of Geothermal Energy Systems**  
*Co-organizer with O. Andersen*  
SIAM Conference on Mathematical & Computational Issues in the Geosciences (GS23), June 19-22, 2023, Bergen, Norway  
Simulation of geothermal energy is a truly multiscale problem: heat is rapidly advected through fractures and high-permeability regions and slowly conducted into the solid rock. This session focused on robust, efficient, and accurate simulation technology for geothermal systems.

## MRST Symposia

**MRST Symposium 2023** (co-chair)  
*Online, September 26-27, 2023*  
The second MRST symposium had over 190 registered participants and consisted of several talks and poster presentations with contributions from the core development team at SINTEF as well as the wider MRST community.

**MRST Symposium 2021** (co-chair)  
*Online, September 14-15, 2021*  
The first MRST Symposium had 169 registered participants and brought together a range of MRST users with many, varying interests with the aim of sharing experiences and inspiring each other in new scientific directions. The symposium consisted of 9 plenary talks, panel discussions, and a poster session featuring 17 different contributions.
