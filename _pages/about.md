My ambition is to fuel the energy transition by enabling better geoenergy systems through numerical simulations. I work as a research scientist in the [applied computational sciences](https://www.sintef.no/en/digital/departments-new/applied-mathematics/computational-geoscience/) group at SINTEF Digital, and my research is in the intersection between mathematics, computer science, and geoscience.

Most of my work centers around development of numerical simulation technology, focusing on efficient linear and nonlinear solution strategies, discretizations, and grid generation. I hold an MSc in industrial mathematics and a PhD in applied mathematics from the [department of mathematical sciences](https://www.ntnu.no/imf) at the Norwegian University of Technology and Science (NTNU). I have also spent time as a visiting student researcher at the institute of [Energy Science & Engineering](https://earth.stanford.edu/ere) at Stanford University during my PhD, and at [KTH Royal Institute of Technology](https://www.kth.se/en) during my MSc.

At this website, you will find information about my research, including publications, talks, teaching, and the occasional blog post. Do not hesitate to reach out!

Software Development
======
I am a firm believer in open and reproducible science, and a large part of my time is devoted to developing high-quality scientific software together with my colleagues in the applied computational sciences group at SINTEF Digital. I am the lead developer of [Fimbul](https://sintefmath.github.io/Fimbul.jl/dev/), an open-source, Julia-based toolbox for geothermal simulation, and I am one of the core developers of [JutulDarcy](https://sintefmath.github.io/JutulDarcy.jl/stable/), our fully differentiable, high-performance porous-media flow simulator. I am also one of the core developers of the [MATLAB Reservoir Simulation Toolbox](https://www.sintef.no/projectweb/mrst/) (MRST), a differentiable, open-source toolbox for rapid prototyping of new models and numerical methods for porous media flow simulation.

Organization
======
I currently serve as the Nordic representative of the Young investigators Committee of the European Community on Computational Methods in Applied Sciences ([ECCOMAS YIC](https://www.eccomas.org/comittees/eyic/)), where I am member of the core group.

I am also part of the organizing committee of the [Geilo Winter Schools in Computational Sciences](https://sintefmath.github.io/geilo-winter-school/), an annual meeting place for PhD students, Postdocs, and young researchers. Hosted at the historical Dr. Holms Hotel, the school gathers leading experts to lecture on emerging topics in eSciences.
