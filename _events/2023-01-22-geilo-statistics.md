The 23rd Geilo Winter School covered the topic of computational statistics, which is of great importance in a number of modern applications. 

The lectures ranged from introductions to Monte Carlo methods, MCMC and friends, Bayesian inference and Integrated Nested Laplace Approximation to using Julia for computational statistics and even covered some reinforcement learning.

**Duration:** January 22-27, 2023

**Role:** Co-organizer
