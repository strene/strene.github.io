The 2026 Geilo Winter School (held January 18–23, 2026) focused on Uncertainty Quantification for Scientific Computing and Intelligent Systems. The program explored the foundations and modern advances of UQ—from Monte Carlo methods and polynomial chaos expansions to Bayesian inference, surrogate modeling, and high‑performance computing—through lectures, hands‑on exercises, and practical tool demonstrations.

**Duration:** January 19-24, 2025

**Lecturers:**
- Hicham Agueny (University of Bergen)
- Kjetil Olsen Lye (SINTEF Digital)
- David Métivier (INRAE Montpellier)
- Anne Reinarz (Durham University)
- Thomas Röblitz (University of Bergen)

**Role:** Main organizer
