Heating and cooling demands almost 50% of the EU's total gross energy consumption. A large portion of this energy can be delivered by direct heat from geothermal resources. 

In this session, we discussed how numerical modeling and data assimilation can help in improving the energy production from direct heat systems.

**Co-organizer:** D. Voskov, Ø. Klemetsdal

**Duration:** June 2024
