Continuous optimization is the study of maximizing functions of continuous variables. Such problems are generally intractable without additional conditions and constraints, but for many specific cases, theoretical research has led to the development of very useful algorithms for practical applications.

Today, such algorithms constitute the workhorse in a diverse range of applications spanning from robotics over machine learning to economics. Recent successes in the field include progress in compressed sensing and advances in large-scale optimization.

In the school of 2022 we looked into a number of exciting topics ranging from continuous optimization basics to topics like shape optimization, PDE-constrained optimization, and the relationship between machine learning and optimization.
