During the fall term of 2022, I served as lecturer and course coordinator for [Algorithms and Data Structures (DATS2300)](https://student.oslomet.no/en/studier/-/studieinfo/emne/DATS2300/2022/H%C3%98ST) at Oslo Metropolitan University. This foundational undergraduate course introduces students to essential algorithms and data structures, focusing on the theoretical analysis, practical design, efficient implementation, and real-world application of commonly used algorithms and data structures in both ordinary and advanced data processing scenarios.

The curriculum covered fundamental topics including:
- Algorithm analysis and complexity theory (Big O notation)
- Linear data structures (arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues)
- Tree structures (binary trees, heaps, balanced trees)
- Hash tables and collision resolution techniques
- Graph algorithms (traversal, shortest paths, minimum spanning trees)
- Sorting and searching algorithms

My responsibilities encompassed comprehensive course management including designing and delivering lectures, supervising compulsory assignments, providing student mentorship during office hours, and designing and grading the comprehensive final examination. The course emphasized both theoretical understanding and practical programming skills in Java.
