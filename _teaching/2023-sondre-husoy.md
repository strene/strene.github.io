Co-supervision of Master's thesis by Sondre Husøy (2023) on "Constrained generation of Voronoi meshes using inscribed sphere distance" at Norwegian University of Science and Technology.

**Main supervisor:** K.-A. Lie  
**Co-supervisors:** Ø. Klemetsdal, A. Johansson

The thesis focused on developing advanced mesh generation techniques using Voronoi diagrams with constraints based on inscribed sphere distance, contributing to improved computational geometry methods for reservoir simulation.
