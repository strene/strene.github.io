Co-supervision of Master's thesis by Duy Duc Khuat (2022) on "Improved optimization methods for adjoint-based training of reduced-order models" at Norwegian University of Science and Technology.

**Main supervisor:** K.-A. Lie  
**Co-supervisors:** S. Krogstad, Ø. Klemetsdal

The thesis investigated advanced optimization techniques for training reduced-order models using adjoint methods, contributing to more efficient computational approaches for large-scale reservoir simulation.
