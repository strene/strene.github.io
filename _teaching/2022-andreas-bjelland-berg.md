Co-supervision of Bachelor's thesis by Andreas Bjelland Berg (2022) on "Combining Gmsh and MRST – Developing software for more efficient grid creation in two dimensions" at Norwegian University of Science and Technology.

**Main supervisor:** K.-A. Lie  
**Co-supervisors:** Ø. Klemetsdal, A. Johansson

The thesis focused on developing software tools that integrate the Gmsh mesh generator with the MATLAB Reservoir Simulation Toolbox (MRST), enabling more efficient grid creation workflows for reservoir simulation applications.
