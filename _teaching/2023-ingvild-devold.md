Co-supervision of Master's thesis by Ingvild S. Devold (2023) on "Graph-based methods for data-driven reservoir modeling" at Norwegian University of Science and Technology.

**Main supervisor:** K.-A. Lie  
**Co-supervisors:** S. Krogstad, Ø. Klemetsdal

The thesis explored innovative graph-based approaches for data-driven reservoir modeling, contributing to more efficient and accurate methods for understanding subsurface flow patterns.
